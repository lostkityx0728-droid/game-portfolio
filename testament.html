01 / THE IDEA

Discover the story through the world.

A text-driven Unity narrative prototype combining conversations, scene exploration and a flight segment.

The player follows Criki and scientist Haruto on a document-delivery journey. NPC conversations and readable objects reveal fragments of the world and its past. The project explores questions of existence and meaning through a hand-sketched visual direction, with chapter and laboratory interaction flows documented alongside the prototype.

CURRENT SCOPE

The portfolio records prototype scenes and dialogue, exploration and flying functions. Inventory-based item delivery is explicitly described as not yet implemented. Six completed chapters, a complete playthrough and persistent save behaviour have not been verified; the flowcharts describe the intended structure.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Connect narrative beats with interaction.

My work covers narrative and interaction design, scene planning and Unity prototype development. The portfolio documents chapter-sequence organisation and dialogue choices, alongside exploration and flying logic. This case presents that development process without treating every planned feature as finished.
