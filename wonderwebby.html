01 / THE IDEA

The project.

A Unity team exploration game for IGB200 / QAGOMA, where a spider builds silk routes through a greenhouse.

( 02 — MY CONTRIBUTION )

From the early idea to connected gameplay.

My work spanned early concept design and core gameplay development in Unity, with a focus on the silk system, hunting and feeding, and the camera and interactions that connect them.

01

Concept & core mechanics

I helped shape the early concept and main play loop: exploration and web building support hunting and feeding, while the routes left by play become the artwork revealed at the end.

02

Silk, hunting & feeding

I developed silk shooting, anchoring and traversal, alongside prey collection and feeding interactions. I integrated these mechanics with the team’s work, with teammate support on feeding.

03

Camera & interaction

I reworked camera behaviour for walls and ceilings, refined collision handling, and added contextual prompts and controller interaction improvements through iteration.
