# Sources and attribution

The collection describes six projects by Rong Guo. Project scope and contribution text were checked against the project materials and team records. Private source repositories, course submissions, participant data and internal documents are not linked or bundled here.

- Wonder Webby is a Unity team project. Official game details: https://alexroper.au/wonderwebby#the-game . Official desktop browser playtest and current screenshots: https://alexropergames.itch.io/wonder-webby . The personal case study focuses on Rong Guo’s verified contributions; the full team roster and team-responsibility detail are not reproduced. Earlier silent prototype clips remain labelled as archived development footage. Private project evidence is not distributed.
- Moon Vagrant screenshots show the exported GDevelop prototype. Its existing artwork and GDevelop watermark are retained. Individual development and playtesting work are described without claiming authorship of every artwork or audio asset. Report suggestions are described as proposals.
- Jiangnan Water Town is an independent academic level prototype inspired by Overwatch. Rong Guo designed its layout and assembled the Unreal environment. Screenshots and the top-down plan come from the authored project materials. Hybrid capture-to-payload progression is a design intention, not a verified complete multiplayer implementation. Unreal starter content and third-party assets, including Traditional Chinese Style Park, remain third-party work. No official Blizzard affiliation is implied.

The visual direction interprets the user-selected reference https://taikisato.com/ with original site code and system fonts. No reference-site code, photographs, biography or project content is included. The compact home introduction is retained.

Moon Vagrant's browser preview was derived from the existing exported game, with the title, comic, combat and ending scenes retained. Official asset-pack metadata: https://resources.gdevelop-app.com/assets-database/assetPacks.json . Runtime licence: https://github.com/4ian/GDevelop/blob/master/GDJS/LICENSE.md . Font licences: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/charm and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/caveat . Sound-effect licence: https://mixkit.co/license/ . The preview contains no complete Wonder Webby team build.

- Testament: selected Unity prototype views, scene sketches and original flow diagrams from the owner-supplied portfolio. The inventory is explicitly unfinished; completed chapters and save persistence are not certified. Literary and game references remain their authors’ work.
- Fukuoka Central Street: selected authored Unreal scene captures and process views from the owner-supplied portfolio. Yakuza 5 streets and Shenmue’s Hazuki Residence are documented references. Assembled third-party character/environment models are not claimed as original modelling work.
- Yakuza — Dragon of Kamurocho: selected physical prototype photographs and a turn-flow excerpt from the owner-supplied portfolio. Yakuza / Like a Dragon provides the setting and character references; Rurik: Dawn of Kiev informs the territory-annexation mechanic. This is an unofficial, non-commercial learning prototype. No print-and-play assets or full source documents are distributed.
