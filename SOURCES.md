# Sources and attribution

The collection describes four projects by Rong Guo. Project scope and contribution text were checked against the project materials and team records. Private source repositories, course submissions, participant data and internal documents are not linked or bundled here.

- Wonder Webby is a team prototype. Existing public portfolio images and silent clips are retained. The main menu UI, visual direction and typography are credited to Leo. Public project background: https://alexroper.au/games/wonder-webby . That page’s personal contributions belong to Alex Roper.
- Moon Vagrant screenshots show the exported GDevelop prototype. Its existing artwork and GDevelop watermark are retained. Individual development and playtesting work are described without claiming authorship of every artwork or audio asset. Report suggestions are described as proposals.
- FanWorks images show Rong’s three Level 2 segment layouts within the team’s Stack O Bot project. Existing mechanics and environment assets are shared content. Selected mechanism checks do not establish uninterrupted completion or measured duration.
- Jiangnan Water Town is an independent academic level prototype inspired by Overwatch. Rong Guo designed its layout and assembled the Unreal environment. Screenshots and the top-down plan come from the authored project materials. Hybrid capture-to-payload progression is a design intention, not a verified complete multiplayer implementation. Unreal starter content and third-party assets, including Traditional Chinese Style Park, remain third-party work. No official Blizzard affiliation is implied.

The visual direction interprets the user-selected reference https://taikisato.com/ with original site code and system fonts. No reference-site code, photographs, biography or project content is included. The compact home introduction is retained.
