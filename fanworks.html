01 / THE IDEA

Make familiar space work differently.

Three industrial puzzle-platforming areas built within the team’s Stack O Bot project.

FanWorks uses retained robot bodies, dependent controls and changing elevation to make players plan their route. Outward journeys become return routes, lifts change access to upper spaces, and simultaneous pressure-plate states govern the final relay.

SEGMENT ROUTES

01

Intake Maintenance

Use a retained body to power airflow, then activate controls along an upper maintenance route.

02

Turbine Crossing

Transfer between rotating blades and landings, then find the elevated return route.

03

Cascade Relay

Keep pressure plates A and B active and reach the master control to enable the exit.

CURRENT SCOPE

The three segments are implemented as prototype layouts. Recorded checks cover selected mechanisms and geometry; continuous traversal from Player Start, first-time navigation and completion time still need playtesting.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Three segments. One spatial language.

I implemented Intake Maintenance, Turbine Crossing and Cascade Relay for Level 2, arranging the routes, vertical layers and local mechanism dependencies.
